# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and
discriminatory or demeaning behavior are not tolerated, in any project space
(issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews).

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior
they deem inappropriate.

**Reporting:** report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer at
<heetae.cho@xcena.com>. Reports will be handled confidentially.

The full text and enforcement guidelines are available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
